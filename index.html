IEEE RAS is the student chapter for robotics and automation — we design autonomous systems, run hands-on build sessions, and send teams to national competitions. No prior robotics experience required; just curiosity and a willingness to solder something at 11pm before a deadline.

01 — ABOUT

A chapter, not a club.

Part of the IEEE Robotics & Automation Society, a global professional body of over 13,000 members. Locally, we're a group of students who'd rather build the thing than just read about it — connecting members with faculty, lab space, and working engineers along the way.

02 — FOCUS AREAS

What we actually work on.

A1

Autonomous Systems

Line-followers, obstacle-avoidance bots, and the control theory that makes them not crash into walls.

A2

Competitive Robotics

All-terrain rovers and combat bots, built for national-level student competitions.

A3

Workshops & Bootcamps

Microcontrollers, embedded C, and ROS basics — taught by senior members, not slides alone.

A4

Industry & Research Links

Guest lectures, lab visits, and mentorship from faculty and working robotics engineers.

03 — PROGRAMS

Flagship events.

SEMESTER · NEW MEMBERS

Build Week

ANNUAL · OPEN COMPETITION
